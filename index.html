04 / Questions

The things people ask
before they stop asking.

Does Hearth use cameras?

No. Hearth uses ambient sensors—temperature, humidity, light and sound—to understand presence. There are no lenses and no video recordings.

Where is my data stored?

On the Hearth hub inside your home. Encrypted at rest. You can export or delete it at any time.

Can I turn it off?

Yes. A single switch on the hub disables sensing and automation. Your home returns to manual control immediately.

What happens if I move?

The hub travels with you. Your home profile can be reset or transferred. The house does not keep a copy.

Is Hearth always listening?

Hearth listens for ambient sound levels, not speech. Audio is processed locally and discarded after the presence reading.
